About Team Fourier

Team Fourier built Tax Reconciliation for the Fintechstico PS2 problem statement: match invoices, bank payments and ledger entries, then find wrong GST, duplicates, missing records and unusual invoices.

What we set out to do

Reconciling GST is slow, manual work: an accountant ties every invoice to a payment and a ledger entry, recomputes the tax, and chases the gaps before filing. We wanted a tool that does the tying and checking automatically, explains every flag in plain words, and proves its accuracy instead of claiming it.

What is in the project

  • Generator: realistic Indian books with GSTINs, the four GST slabs, and planted errors with an answer key.
  • Engine: duplicate detection, exact and fuzzy payment matching, ledger matching, GST and record checks, an Isolation Forest anomaly score, and the tax position.
  • Dashboard: flagged records with evidence and next steps, record detail side by side, tax totals for reports, accuracy, verification, and Ask the Ledger.
  • Measurement: precision and recall for every error type, against the answer key, on every run.

Built with

Python, Flask, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, RapidFuzz, Plotly and openpyxl; Flask-Login and SQLite for accounts; Claude for Ask the Ledger; GSAP for page transitions.
