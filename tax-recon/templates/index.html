Smart Tax Reconciliation

Tax errors, caught. Match invoices, bank payments and ledger entries. Find wrong GST, duplicates, missing records and unusual invoices — with accuracy you can measure, not just claim.

Open Dashboard See Accuracy

What we do

Match

Link each invoice to its bank payment and ledger entry — exact match on IDs, fuzzy match on name, amount and date.

Check

Recompute GST on every invoice, find duplicates, and flag missing records across all three sources.

Flag

Machine learning scores every invoice for how unusual it looks — catches patterns no rule can describe.
